Mission (Manifesto)

It is, in fact, no longer an argument that the rise of LLMs and agents is transforming the way we build things. Skills and intelligence are now available for just a few dollars per token.

What a wonderful and glorious time to witness such possibilities and the rate at which individuals and organizations are building new systems and modifying existing ones.

With this great capacity comes greater responsibility. The core fundamentals of building a product remain the same: idea to development to production. This new era enables development at an incredible speed, but even with that, many organizations remain stuck in an infinite loop of development, iteration, and experimentation, sometimes ending up with something that is ultimately discarded.

The gospel of intelligent systems has now spread to industry and domain experts across different sectors, encouraging them to think about how they can make their systems more intelligent. Some have already spent significant time experimenting, iterating, and working with different agents to figure out how to get things done. While these experiments may start cheaply, the cost of tokens can grow rapidly as the cycle of prompting, testing, regenerating, and refining continues. What begins as an inexpensive experiment can quickly become a costly and time-consuming development loop without a clear path to production..

At BrassinAI, we strongly believe that domain expertise is essential when moving from an idea to a real product. And that is where we come in.

Industry experts in any sector should not have to stress themselves trying to understand every part of an AI system or how to integrate it. They already have deep knowledge of their industries, problems, and existing systems. We bring the technical expertise in AI.

With our strong knowledge and experience in deep learning, machine learning for tabular data, AI infrastructure, and intelligent systems, we can iterate faster and save organizations the endless time spent researching, experimenting, and delaying their path to production.

At BrassinAI, we also research gaps and overlooked opportunities in the field, identifying how they can be leveraged to improve the everyday experiences of developers, businesses, and users.

How We Work

BrassinAI comprises experts across different AI domains, each with solid years of experience in corporate environments, open source communities, and competitive data science. BrassinAI is structured in such a way that each of these experts can take full ownership of the core systems we operate in.

To Business

We dedicate significant time to meeting and aligning with our business customers. We do not just try to understand what they want us to do. We aim to be brutally honest about the feasibility of the problem, understanding why the problem exists, what is broken, and what makes up the system they are currently operating in.

We also take time to absorb their domain knowledge, where we believe they are the experts, and then refine that understanding to determine how our AI domain expertise can help reshape or improve the system before we even get started.

Our overall aim is not just to deliver, but to make sure the problem is properly understood and that our solution is implemented in a way that creates a visible turnaround for the customer's business.

To Product

At the core of BrassinAI is helping other organizations and individuals achieve their dream products. Nevertheless, BrassinAI also embraces building its own products, which can take different forms, including open source, B2B, or user-facing products.

These products may either aim to solve everyday user problems or create tools that help developers avoid infinite loops of implementation caused by the rapidly changing nature of AI development.

We also aim to remain as lean an organization as possible. Therefore, every product that emerges from BrassinAI can take different forms of ownership:

  • Partnership: BrassinAI can create a product and hand it over to partners to fully manage, or a product can be built from the onset together with partners, with ownership divided between both parties.
  • Standalone Entity: BrassinAI can also choose to manage a product as a separate entity from BrassinAI itself, with its own complete organizational structure.

BrassinAI may or may not be involved in the day-to-day operations of such an entity. By this, we mean that the entity can have its own executives who are separate from the BrassinAI team, while BrassinAI may still retain some executive decision-making authority regarding certain upstream or strategic actions.
